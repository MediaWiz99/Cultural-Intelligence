🌍 How Culturally Intelligent Are You?

In today's global business environment, technical skills alone aren't enough. Cultural Intelligence (CQ) — the capability to function effectively across cultural contexts — has become a critical competency for managers.

This assessment measures your CQ across four dimensions developed by researchers Soon Ang and Linn Van Dyne, helping you understand your strengths and identify opportunities to increase your cross-cultural effectiveness.

— Soon Ang & Linn Van Dyne, Handbook of Cultural Intelligence (2008)
